# CISP 2026 Steward Board

## Role

Stewards are independent experts who oversee CISP challenge integrity.
They are NOT affiliated with PWM development and have no stake in outcomes.

## Responsibilities

1. **Validate challenge design**: Review track definitions, mismatch conditions,
   and scoring criteria before sealed data release
2. **Verify results**: Independently re-score top-N submissions using sealed data
3. **Adjudicate disputes**: Resolve appeals within 14 days (see `rules.md`)
4. **Audit integrity**: Verify RunBundle SHA-256 hashes, budget declarations,
   and isolation compliance
5. **Publish report**: Issue public steward report after results announcement

## Governance

- **Quorum**: 2 of 3 stewards required for any official decision
- **Recusal**: Stewards must recuse if affiliated with a competing team
- **Term**: 2 years, renewable once
- **Removal**: By unanimous vote of remaining stewards + PWM core team
- **Compensation**: Co-authorship on CISP proceedings, travel support for workshop

## Conflict of Interest Policy

A steward has a conflict of interest if:
- They are a co-author on a competing submission
- They are employed by the same institution as a competing team lead
- They have a financial relationship with a competing entity
- They have co-published with a competing team lead in the past 12 months

Conflicted stewards must disclose the conflict and recuse from scoring
the relevant submission.

## Communication

- Steward deliberations are confidential until results announcement
- Steward reports are published alongside CISP results
- Stewards may issue errata or corrections within 30 days of results
