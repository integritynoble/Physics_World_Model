## 1. Physics & Forward Model

**Modality:** Scanning Acoustic Microscopy (SAM) — also called ultrasonic C-scan imaging

**Physical principle:** SAM uses focused high-frequency ultrasound (10 MHz – 2 GHz) to image subsurface features in layered electronic packages, CFRP composites, and biological tissue. A piezoelectric transducer generates a pulse focused through a sapphire lens onto the sample in a coupling fluid (deionised water). The acoustic wave reflects from acoustic-impedance discontinuities (delaminations: R ≈ −0.8; voids: R ≈ −0.95; inclusions: R ≈ +0.5), and the gate-selected reflected intensity is recorded as the lens is raster-scanned to form a 2-D C-scan image. At high frequencies (100–1000 MHz), lateral resolution reaches 1–20 µm.

**Forward model:**
```
r(x, y) = [R(·) * h_PSF](x, y) + n(x, y)

where:
  R(x, y)   — true acoustic reflectivity map (ground truth, range [−1, +1])
  h_PSF     — acoustic PSF of the focused lens: Gaussian with
              FWHM_lateral ≈ 0.51 λ / NA  (λ = c_s / f_0)
  *         — 2-D convolution
  n(x, y)   — Gaussian detector noise (+ quantisation)

Reflectivity at an interface:
  R = (Z_2 − Z_1) / (Z_2 + Z_1),  where Z_k = ρ_k · c_s,k
```

**Inverse problem:** Recover the 2-D acoustic reflectivity map R(x,y) from PSF-blurred C-scan measurements r(x,y), given uncertain knowledge of the acoustic PSF (sound speed, lens NA, transducer frequency).

## 2. Mismatch Parameters & Benchmark Structure

**Spec notation:** P(ultrasound pulse) → S(coupling fluid, sound speed c_s) → D(piezo lens, f_0, NA)

**Key mismatch parameters:**
- `sound_speed` (c_s): water sound speed; nominal 1480 m/s, range 1450–1520 m/s (temperature variation)
- `center_frequency` (f_0): transducer frequency; nominal 100 MHz, range 50–500 MHz (determines PSF width)
- `attenuation_coeff` (α): coupling-fluid attenuation; nominal 0.5 dB/(mm·MHz), range 0.5–3.0
- `lens_na` (NA): numerical aperture; nominal 0.6, range 0.4–0.9 (determines lateral resolution)

**Dataset format:**
- `x_true: (256, 256)` — 2-D acoustic reflectivity map in [0, 1] (normalised); features: die-attach boundary, elliptical delaminations, circular voids, inclusion spots. Generated by `generate_sam_phantom()`.
- `y: (256, 256)` — PSF-convolved measurement with added Gaussian noise at target SNR.
- `H_ideal: (256, 256, 256, 256)` — ideal PSF convolution operator.

**Generator:** `generate_sam_phantom()` in `benchmarks/datasets/downloaders.py` — produces physics-calibrated SAM C-scan reflectivity maps with die-attach boundaries, delaminations (R≈−0.6), voids (R≈−0.9), and inclusion/wire-bond pads (R≈+0.5). Calibrated to Guo et al. (2022) and Rigby et al. (2023).

## 3. Reconstruction Methods & Leaderboard

| Algorithm | Type | Reference | Appropriateness |
|-----------|------|-----------|-----------------|
| SAFT | Classical | Schickert et al., NDT&E Int. 36:339, 2003 | Synthetic aperture focusing — the gold-standard classical method for ultrasonic C-scan image formation; baseline for all comparisons |
| Wiener Deconv | Classical | Zinin et al., J. Appl. Phys. 1997 | Wiener filter deconvolution of the acoustic PSF; widely used in SAM image restoration |
| PnP-ADMM | Plug-and-Play | Venkatakrishnan et al., IEEE GlobalSIP 2013 | PnP-ADMM with learned denoiser; state-of-the-art plug-and-play for inverse problems with known PSF |
| SAM-Net | Deep Learning | Guo et al., Ultrasonics 122:106679, 2022 | CNN trained on real SAM images of microelectronic packages; domain-specific deep learning baseline |
| Self-Sup Deconv | Self-Supervised | He et al., IEEE Trans. Instrum. Meas. 73, 2024 | Blind deconvolution using Stein's unbiased risk estimator — no ground-truth required for training |
| PINN-SAM | Physics-Informed | Guo et al., IEEE UFFC 71:340, 2024 | Physics-informed NN embedding acoustic propagation equation into the loss function |
| AcousticFormer | Transformer | Zhu et al., Ultrasonics 138:107212, 2024 | Vision Transformer for high-frequency SAM (1 GHz); outperforms DAS at sub-micron scale |
| DiffusionSAM | Diffusion | Score-based diffusion for SAM, 2024 | Score-based diffusion posterior sampling; provides uncertainty estimates on delamination extent |

## 4. Literature & State of the Art (2024–2025)

1. **Guo, S. et al.** "Deep learning-based scanning acoustic microscopy image quality enhancement." *Ultrasonics* 122:106679, 2022. — CNN trained on real SAM images of semiconductor packages; demonstrates 5 dB PSNR gain over SAFT for delamination detection.

2. **Rigby, S. et al.** "Deep learning for scanning acoustic microscopy defect detection." *NDT&E International* 138:102871, 2023. — ResNet classifier + C-scan reconstruction for through-package void and delamination detection; state-of-the-art on ENAC benchmark.

3. **He, Y. et al.** "Self-supervised deconvolution for scanning acoustic microscopy using Stein's unbiased risk estimator." *IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement* 73:1–12, 2024. — Blind deconvolution without ground-truth labels using SURE; practical for unknown lens PSFs in field instruments.

4. **Zhu, C. et al.** "Transformer-based beamforming for high-frequency scanning acoustic microscopy." *Ultrasonics* 138:107212, 2024. — Vision Transformer for 1 GHz SAM image formation; surpasses DAS by 3.5 dB PSNR and achieves sub-micron defect localisation in CFRP laminates.
