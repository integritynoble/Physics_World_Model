## 1. Physics & Forward Model

**Modality:** X-ray Angiography (Digital Subtraction Angiography / 3D Rotational Angiography)

**Physical principle:** X-ray angiography visualizes blood vessels by injecting an iodinated contrast agent (typically 300–370 mg I/mL, e.g., Omnipaque) and acquiring X-ray projection images. In digital subtraction angiography (DSA), a "mask" image acquired before injection is logarithmically subtracted from post-contrast "filled" images to isolate vessel contrast against soft tissue background. In 3D rotational angiography (3DRA), a C-arm rotates over a ~180°–220° arc at ~30 fps acquiring cone-beam projections; a 3D vessel volume is reconstructed via FDK/CBCT algorithms.

**Forward model:**
```
y_i = I_0 · exp( -∫ [μ_soft(x,E) + μ_I(x,E)] dl ) + n_i

DSA subtraction approximation (monochromatic, small-iodine limit):
  y_DSA ≈ μ_I(E) · ∫ c_I(x) dl  +  n
         ≈ μ_I · (iodine line integral)

3DRA forward operator (Radon / cone-beam projection):
  y = R(θ) x + n,   y ∈ R^{N_views × N_det}

where:
  x        — 3D iodine attenuation map (vessel tree, ground truth)
  R(θ)     — cone-beam Radon projector at angle θ (Feldkamp geometry)
  μ_I(E)   — iodine mass-attenuation coefficient (~5 cm²/g at 80 kVp)
  I_0      — incident photon flux (dose level)
  n        — Poisson detector noise at clinical dose
```

**Inverse problem:** Recover the vessel attenuation/iodine-concentration map x from a set of projection measurements y, given uncertain knowledge of the acquisition parameters (kVp, scatter fraction, patient motion, contrast timing).

## 2. Mismatch Parameters & Benchmark Structure

**Spec notation:** Π(proj) → D(g, η₁)

**Key mismatch parameters:**
- `contrast_timing` (Δt_c): contrast bolus timing error; nominal 0 s, perturbed ±0.5 s
- `motion` (σ_m): patient motion amplitude; nominal 0 mm, perturbed 2.0 mm
- `scatter` (f_s): scatter-to-primary ratio error; nominal 0, perturbed 0.05

**Dataset format:**
- `x_true: (128, 128)` — 2D projection of iodine concentration map; fractal vascular tree with main trunk + 2-order branches using Murray's law radius tapering. Generated by `generate_angiography_vessel_phantom()`.
- `y: (180, 182)` — Radon sinogram (180 projection angles × 182 detector pixels) with Poisson noise at clinical dose levels.
- `H_ideal: (180,)` — projection angles vector for ideal Radon operator.

**Generator:** `generate_angiography_vessel_phantom()` in `benchmarks/datasets/downloaders.py` — produces physics-calibrated vascular tree phantoms with:
- Near-vertical main trunk (aorta/ICA) with mild tortuosity; radius 8–15 px using Gaussian cross-section profiles.
- 2–4 first-order branches at 30–60° angles with Murray's law taper (r^3 = const at bifurcations).
- 1–3 second-order branches per first-order vessel with decreasing contrast (~40–65% of parent amplitude) to model physiological iodine dilution.
- Slight Gaussian smoothing (σ=0.8 px) to simulate X-ray focal spot blur.
Calibrated to DSA/3DRA physics: Shen et al. (2024) and Wang et al. (2024).

## 4. Literature & State of the Art (2024–2025)

1. **Shen, C. et al.** "Geometry-aware diffusion model for few-view angiography reconstruction." *Medical Image Analysis* 94:103102, 2024. — Score-based diffusion prior conditioned on cone-beam projection geometry; achieves SOTA at 60-view 3DRA with 4 dB PSNR gain over TV-ADMM; the definitive 2024 SOTA for angiography-specific diffusion models.

2. **Wang, Z. et al.** "Motion-compensated angiography reconstruction with implicit neural representation." *IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging* 43:1401, 2024. — Continuous implicit neural field jointly estimating vessel structure and cardiac motion; reduces motion artifacts in 4D DSA by 35% (SSF metric) vs. rigid-body correction.

3. **Zhang, Y. et al.** "Vessel-preserving deep learning for digital subtraction angiography enhancement." *Radiology: AI* 6:e230298, 2024. — UNet-based model trained on paired DSA data; achieves significant noise reduction while preserving small vessel detail better than TV methods; validated on Siemens Artis One and Philips Allura Clarity.

4. **Tian, L. et al.** "Self-supervised low-dose angiography reconstruction via forward model consistency." *arXiv:2411.08234*, 2024. — Self-supervised framework requiring only noisy measurements; achieves near-supervised performance enabling deployment without clean reference images.
