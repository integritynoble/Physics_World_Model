How to describe a modality

Simply tell the AI what imaging system you want. It understands all 168 modalities — from everyday (CT, MRI) to specialized (ptychography, ghost imaging).

1
Name the modality: “Design a confocal microscope”
2
Add constraints: “...with 40x objective and 488nm laser”
3
Specify noise: “...with Poisson shot noise”

How to fine-tune a spec

After the AI generates a spec, send follow-up messages to refine it:

Tune mismatch parameters

“Set the center-of-rotation offset to 2.0 pixels and show the impact”

Modify the forward model

“Add a wavelength dispersion primitive before the detector”

Add new mismatch params

“Add detector gain mismatch with nominal=1.0 and perturbed=1.05”

Change noise model

“Switch to mixed Poisson-Gaussian noise with read noise 5e-”

Simplify the pipeline

“Remove the structured illumination step and explain the tradeoff”

Dataset Mode

Upload your own measurement data and reconstruct using PWM's best methods:

1
Upload: Click "Upload Dataset" and select your .npy/.mat/.h5 file
2
Describe: “This is CT sinogram data from a fan-beam scanner”
3
Refine: Adjust noise model, mismatch params to match your system
4
Reconstruct: Click "Reconstruct from Dataset" to apply the best method
