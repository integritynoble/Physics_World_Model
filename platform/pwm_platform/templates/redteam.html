Red-Team Dashboard

Adversarial challenges against published claims — find reproducibility failures, dataset contamination, or physics violations to earn Red-Team badges. See Red-Team Contributor role for details.

Red-Team Contributor workflow:
  1. Pick an open bounty or target a specific Certified-tier claim from the leaderboard.
  2. Run adversarial evaluation locally with pwm evaluate --adversarial.
  3. Document your finding: exact failure mode, reproduction steps, affected claim ID.
  4. Submit your finding report as a claim via /claims (scaffold a new claim with method = "red-team-finding").
  5. Admins review and, if valid, revoke or downgrade the affected claim.
