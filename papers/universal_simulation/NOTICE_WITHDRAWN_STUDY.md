# Notice: the "prospective benchmark" was not a real study

Earlier versions of this folder contained a "prospective benchmark" of 72 blinded tasks attributed to 12 external scientists and an independent coordinator. Three files described it: `data/table2_prospective.json`, `zenodo_archive/benchmark_tasks/72_prospective_tasks.json` and `ALL_72_TASKS_SPEC.md`.

**That study was not conducted.** No external scientists or coordinator took part, and no runs of those 72 tasks were made. The records were written to match a draft of the paper. The named people and the per-task outcomes are not real, and nobody named in them was involved. The three files have been removed.

The author is correcting the related arXiv paper (2603.25780) accordingly. Only results backed by archived run records will be reported.

— Chengshuai Yang, 22 October 2026
