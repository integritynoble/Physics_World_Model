\section*{Paper Overview}

This paper introduces \textbf{Physics World Models (PWM)}, a universal diagnostic and correction framework built on the \textbf{Triad Decomposition}, which attributes imaging failures to three gates: recoverability (Gate~1), carrier budget (Gate~2), and operator mismatch (Gate~3).  The \textbf{OperatorGraph IR} encodes forward models across modalities into a common DAG formalism, enabling modality-agnostic diagnosis.

\paragraph{Central finding.}  Gate~3 (operator mismatch) dominates in \emph{every} validated modality.  A sub-pixel mask perturbation erases \textbf{twice the reconstruction gains} of a decade of solver innovation.

\section*{Sections Most Relevant to Your Expertise}

\subsection*{1. CASSI Deep Dive: GAP-TV, MST-L, HDNet Under Mismatch}

The paper uses the 5-parameter InverseNet mismatch ($dx{=}0.5$\,px, $dy{=}0.3$\,px, $\theta{=}0.1^\circ$, $a_1{=}2.02$, $\alpha{=}0.15^\circ$) across 10 KAIST scenes:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{Sc.~I} & \textbf{Sc.~II} & \textbf{Sc.~IV} & \textbf{$\rho$} \\
\midrule
GAP-TV  & $24.34$ & $20.96$ & $21.72$ & 0.22 \\
MST-L   & $34.81$ & $20.83$ & $27.33$ & 0.46 \\
HDNet   & $34.66$ & $21.88$ & $21.88$ & 0.00 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Key insight:} All solvers collapse to a narrow Scenario~II range ($20.83$--$21.88$\,dB), confirming the failure is operator-driven.  GAP-TV achieves $\rho = 0.22$ recovery; MST-L achieves $\rho = 0.46$ because it conditions on the mask.  HDNet's mask-oblivious architecture shows $\rho = 0$---it cannot exploit a corrected mask.

\subsection*{2. CACTI Results: EfficientSCI and Temporal Compression}

CACTI exhibits the most severe mismatch sensitivity among photon-domain modalities:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{Sc.~I} & \textbf{Sc.~II} & \textbf{Sc.~III} & \textbf{$\rho$} \\
\midrule
GAP-TV        & $26.75$ & $15.81$ & $26.01$ & $0.93$ \\
PnP-FFDNet    & $29.28$ & $11.43$ & $25.39$ & $0.78$ \\
ELP-Unfolding & $34.09$ & $15.47$ & $29.40$ & $0.75$ \\
EfficientSCI  & $35.39$ & $14.81$ & $27.38$ & $0.61$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Key insights:} All four methods suffer catastrophic mismatch degradation ($-10.94$ to $-20.58$\,dB), confirming operator-driven failure.  GAP-TV achieves the highest recovery ratio ($\rho = 0.93$), recovering $+10.21$\,dB of the $10.94$\,dB degradation---nearly perfect calibration.  EfficientSCI has the lowest $\rho$ ($0.61$) despite the best Sc.~I performance, indicating that deep unrolling networks suffer more \emph{irreversible} damage from mismatch than iterative methods.  PnP-FFDNet and ELP-Unfolding fall in between ($\rho = 0.78$ and $0.75$).  The temporal mask replication amplifies calibration errors multiplicatively across all compressed frames.

\subsection*{3. SPC Gain Mismatch: A Qualitatively Different Failure Mode}

SPC presents \textbf{gain bias} rather than geometric shift:

\begin{itemize}
  \item 5\% detector gain drift $\Rightarrow$ $-12.21$\,dB PSNR drop
  \item PWM diagnoses Gate~3 localized to detector gain node
  \item Correction recovers the full $12.21$\,dB ($\rho = 1.0$)
  \item \textbf{But}: Gain-type mismatch is \emph{not} recoverable from measurement residual alone (Scenario~IV recovery = 0\% for SPC), because gain bias scales signal and noise uniformly
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{4. Real-Data Hardware Validation}

\paragraph{CASSI real (TSA dataset, 5 scenes):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item GAP-TV residual ratio: $1.8\times$ (consistent across 5 scenes, range $1.6$--$2.0\times$)
  \item HDNet: $1.0\times$ (mask-oblivious, insensitive)
  \item MST-S/MST-L: ${\sim}1.0\times$ on real data (vs.\ $-13.98$\,dB in simulation!)
  \item \textbf{Explanation}: Real hardware mask contains pre-existing manufacturing errors; additional $0.5$\,px perturbation is small relative to existing imperfections
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{CACTI real (4 scenes):}
\begin{itemize}
  \item GAP-TV residual ratio: $10.4\times$ (range $9.4$--$11.0\times$)
  \item PnP-FFDNet: $2.0\times$ (deep prior provides partial robustness)
  \item Temporal compression amplifies errors across all 10 frames
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Autonomous calibration:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item CASSI: Grid search recovers 78\% of oracle correction (1,234\,s)
  \item CACTI: Grid search recovers \textbf{100\%} of oracle correction (64\,s)
  \item SPC: 0\% recovery (gain mismatch invisible to residual-based calibration)
\end{itemize}
